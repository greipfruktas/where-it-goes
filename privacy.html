WHERE IT GOES

Privacy

Effective 25 September 2026

Your personal expenses

Personal expenses are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to our shared-group database unless you deliberately add an expense inside a shared group.

Shared groups

Shared group names, membership, expenses, splits and repayments are stored in Supabase so invited group members can see and update the same trip or event. This information is available only to signed-in members of that group.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in provides the basic account information needed to identify you, such as your name, email address and profile image. We use it only for signing you in and showing who added or owes a shared expense. We do not receive your Google password.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Shared data is processed by Supabase and Google only as needed to provide authentication and group syncing.

Deleting your data

You can remove personal expenses inside the app. To delete your account or shared-group data, contact the app owner using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen.
