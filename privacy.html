WHERE IT GOES

Privacy

Effective 2 October 2026

Your personal expenses

You can use Personal without an account; those guest expenses stay on that device. If you sign in with Google, your Personal expenses, categories, learned category rules and selected style are privately synchronized through Supabase so they remain available on your other signed-in devices. Group members cannot see your Personal data.

Bank statement imports

Your bank statement file is read inside the app and stays on your device. The raw workbook, incoming payments, balances and account details are not uploaded. If you are signed in, only the saved expenses and learned category rules sync with your private Personal data.

Shared groups

Shared group names, membership, expenses, splits and repayments are stored in Supabase so invited group members can see and update the same trip or event. This information is available only to signed-in members of that group.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in provides the basic account information needed to identify you, such as your name, email address and profile image. We use it for private Personal syncing and for showing who added or owes a shared expense. We do not receive your Google password.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Data is processed by Supabase and Google only as needed to provide authentication and syncing. A local cached copy may remain on a device after sign-out so the app can restore guest data and work offline.

Deleting your data

You can remove personal expenses inside the app. To delete your account or shared-group data, contact the app owner using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen.
